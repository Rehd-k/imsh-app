# Prompt 12 — Emergency request

```
Same IMSH Vivid Care design system as the previous screens. Native iOS iPhone 15, 390×844. OVERLY colorful. Ambulance / ER / triage photos. Motion. Native app. No tab bar.

Screen: Create emergency request (signed-in). High stakes, still Vivid Care: loud coral, living photos, huge controls. Not a beige form. Not a horror scene. No blood.

TOP: back in a pulsing coral circle. Title “Request help” in magenta extra-bold with a tiny bouncing ambulance. A thin pulse-ring animation around the title.

BACKGROUND: photo of an ambulance bay at Ibom Specialist-style hospital at dusk, overlaid with coral-magenta mesh. Floating bubbles: ER doors, paramedic, stretcher (tasteful, West African staff).

INTRO on a sunshine speech sticker: “Share where you are and what is wrong. The desk sees this immediately.”

BODY, huge gaps, huge targets:

1) LOCATION CARD — aqua-to-lime gradient, mini map thumbnail of Uyo plus a PHOTO of a street in Uyo. Lime pulse pin. “Udo Street, off Oron Road, Uyo”. “GPS ~12 m”. Button “Update location” with a bouncing satellite. Annotate the pin pulsing.

2) DESCRIBE — a big coral-border notepad on peach, 5 lines, sample “Sudden chest pain and shortness of breath. Started 10 minutes ago. I am at home.” Mic-shaped cursor bounce.

3) ATTACH as three STORY TILES in a row (they are slides you can tap):
   - Voice: photo of a paramedic radio, magenta, waveform animation, “0:00 / 2:00”
   - Video: photo of a phone recording in a living room, violet, “30 seconds”
   - Text: lime check sticker “Text added”, photo of a handwritten note
   Caption orange sticker: “Send at least one of text, voice, or video.”

4) SAFETY: electric-blue tape “Also call if you can. This does not replace a phone call.”

PINNED BOTTOM: 60pt MAGENTA-CORAL gradient pill “Send emergency request” with THREE pulse rings and a shimmer. Ambulance icon bouncing. This is the loudest button in the whole app.

Annotate: pulse everywhere on emergency actions, tiles scale on press, background slow zoom. No siren illustrations, no flashing seizure-risk strobes — pulse and shimmer only, 1.5s breath.
```
